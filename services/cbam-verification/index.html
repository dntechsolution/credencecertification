Climate, Carbon & Sustainability Assurance

CBAM Readiness & Pre-Verification Services

Independent readiness assessment and pre-verification of embedded emissions data for Indian exporters to the EU and UK.

The EU's Carbon Border Adjustment Mechanism (CBAM) entered its definitive period on 1 January 2026. EU importers now pay for the carbon embedded in covered goods, and they can only use an exporter's actual emissions figures if those figures have been independently verified. Credence Certification Services Private Limited helps manufacturers and exporters turn their emissions data into structured, audit-ready evidence, so that buyers can rely on it and you are not forced onto higher default values.

Accreditation Scope Note

Under EU CBAM rules, final verification of actual emissions for an importer's declaration must be conducted by a verifier accredited by an EU/EEA National Accreditation Body. Credence currently provides independent CBAM readiness assessments and pre-verification data checks while preparing for applicable third-country accreditation. Accredited verification reports are offered only upon grant of applicable EU NAB accreditation.

Commercial urgency

Why CBAM readiness & pre-verification matters.

Every shipment produced in 2026 feeds into your buyer's first annual CBAM declaration. Waiting until 2027 leaves gaps when records can no longer be reconstructed.

01 / COST PROTECTION

Avoid the default-value penalty.

Where actual data is not verified, importers fall back on Commission default values, which carry a mark-up that rises every year (+10% in 2026, +20% in 2027, +30% from 2028). That cost lands on your buyer and, in turn, on your price competitiveness.

02 / MARKET ACCESS

Win and keep EU/UK buyers.

Audit-ready, verified data gives European and UK importers the confidence to place long-term orders and reduces supply-chain friction at customs and declaration time.

03 / TRACEABILITY

Build supply-chain transparency.

A documented, traceable data trail from plant to port is now a commercial requirement, not an extra—especially when precursor inputs cross multiple suppliers.

04 / EFFICIENCY

Find data gaps and reduction opportunities.

Pre-verification exposes weak metering, missing calibration records or boundary errors early, and highlights where emissions—and therefore carbon cost—can be cut.

05 / DEADLINE WATCH

Stay ahead of the clock.

The first annual CBAM declaration, covering 2026 imports, is due by 30 September 2027. Data is being generated in your plant today—starting readiness checks now prevents last-minute default penalties.

Scope of support

Seven structured deliverables designed to make your installation's emissions data consistent, traceable and ready for formal CBAM verification.

01 · Classification

Product applicability and CN code review

Process mapping and data checks

CBAM Readiness Checklist

For Indian exporters preparing 2026 embedded emissions data ahead of the 30 September 2027 EU CBAM declaration.

  1. 01. Product & CN Code Mapping

    Verify 8-digit Combined Nomenclature (CN) codes for all exported goods and confirm CBAM applicability & aggregated goods categories.

  2. 02. Installation & Production Route Boundaries

    Document installation boundaries, production processes, fuel inputs, process emissions and mass-balance reconciliation.

  3. 03. Metering & Calibration Records

    Ensure fuel flow meters, weighbridges and electricity sub-meters are calibrated, logged and cross-checked against invoices.

  4. 04. Precursor Material Data

    Collect embedded emissions records and supplier communication data for purchased precursors (e.g. clinker, hot-metal, billets, alumina).

  5. 05. Electricity Consumption & Emission Factors

    Separate grid, captive and renewable/PPA power consumption and retain supporting utility bills, PPA contracts and emission-factor evidence.

  6. 06. Importer Communication Template

    Validate that data shared with EU/UK buyers matches CBAM communication template fields with full traceability back to plant records.
