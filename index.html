The assurance charter

CCS / 01
“Our independence is what makes our certificate mean something.”

What remains separate

Consulting, advisory and implementation support are never provided to organisations we certify.

Credence Certification Services is an independent Certification Body providing accredited third-party certification, verification and assessment services across quality, food safety, environment, energy and social-compliance domains.

IND / Independence01

No conflicted advice.

We do not provide consulting, advisory or implementation support to any organisation we certify.

CMP / Competence02

Qualified judgement.

Lead Auditors and technical reviewers are assessed against documented competence criteria.

IMP / Impartiality03

Decisions protected.

An independent committee protects every certification decision from commercial influence.

ACC / Accessibility04

Built for access.

Our systems serve enterprises, FPOs, MSMEs, agri-processors and export-oriented producers.

Certification process

Eight accountable decisions.

A connected path from application to recertification, with independent review built into every critical hand-off.

  1. 01

    Application

    Scope confirmation and impartiality screening.

  2. 02

    Stage 1 audit

    Document and readiness review.

  3. 03

    Stage 2 audit

    Implementation and effectiveness assessment.

  4. 04

    Technical review

    Independent review of audit findings.

  5. 05

    Decision

    Impartial certification approval.

  6. 06

  7. 07

    Surveillance

Engagement model

Assignment-based roles with no full-time commitment required. Engagements are compensated per audit man-day or assignment day.

Credence Certification Services Pvt. Ltd. is an independent Certification Body being established to provide accredited third-party certification, verification and assessment services across Quality, Food Safety, Environment, Energy and Social Compliance domains—free of any consulting, advisory or implementation relationship with the organisations it certifies.

As part of building our operational readiness for accreditation by the National Accreditation Board for Certification Bodies (NABCB), CCS invites applications from qualified professionals for empanelment as Lead Auditors and Technical Experts.

Why empanel with us

  • 01

    Assignment-based engagement—work on your own terms, with no full-time commitment required.

  • 02

    Exposure to a growing portfolio across food processing, agri-value chains, energy and industrial sectors.

  • 03

    A structured, impartial audit environment built to ISO/IEC 17021-1 requirements.

  • 04

    Transparent, competence-based selection and fair man-day compensation.

Conduct QMS audits across client sectors—audit planning, execution, reporting and certification recommendations—in line with ISO 9001:2015 and NABCB accreditation requirements.

Engagement

Empanelled on a contract basis and compensated per audit man-day. Full-time opportunities may follow based on assignment volume.

Eligibility

  • ✓IRCA / Exemplar Global certified QMS Lead Auditor (ISO 9001:2015)

  • ✓Minimum 5 years' industry experience with at least 3 years in QMS auditing
  • ✓Meets NABCB's Lead Auditor competence criteria for the QMS scheme
FSMS / 02 Lead Auditor—Food Safety Management SystemsISO 22000 / FSSC 22000

Conduct FSMS audits across the food and feed supply chain, including HACCP plan verification and PRP assessment, in line with ISO 22000:2018 / FSSC 22000 requirements.

Engagement

Empanelled on a contract basis and compensated per audit man-day. Full-time opportunities may follow based on assignment volume.

Eligibility

  • ✓IRCA / Exemplar Global certified FSMS Lead Auditor (ISO 22000:2018)
  • ✓HACCP certification (mandatory)
  • ✓FSSC 22000 scheme-specific training preferred
  • ✓Bachelor's/Master's in Food Technology, Food Science, Dairy Technology or an allied field
  • ✓Minimum 5 years' food industry / food safety experience
TECH / 03 Technical Expert—Food Processing & Agri SectorSector-specific technical input

Provide sector-specific technical input to audit teams on specialised processes such as food-processing lines, cold chain and agri-input formulation, supporting document review and technical evaluation.

Engagement

Empanelled on-call and compensated per assignment day.

Eligibility

  • ✓Minimum 8–10 years' relevant technical/industry experience
  • ✓Working familiarity with GMP/GHP and sector-specific hazards and regulations
